# 18 · In your own words: the real material letters and answers are built from

`knowledge-base/17-writing.md` says how to use this: pick one or two entries that fit the employer
and retell them, shorter, the way you told them.

**Rules for using it.**

- Retell it. Don't paste it in word for word, and don't polish it into a slogan.
- A number or a fact also has to be in `04-experience-detail.md` or `07-master-resume.md`. Where
  they differ, those files win.
- Say who someone was the way you'd say it. Don't name a person unless they've agreed to it.
- Nothing fits this employer? Write less. Never make up a story, a reason or a feeling to fill the gap.
